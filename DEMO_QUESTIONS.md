# Vitreon Legal Demo Questions

10 questions for sales calls and demos. Each is drawn from the DIFC corpus.
Expected answer quality: cited, authoritative, 400-650 characters.

---

## 1. Limitation Periods

**Question:** What is the limitation period for a contract claim under DIFC Law No. 5 of 2005?

**Why it works:** Crisp factual answer, clear citation to a specific law, demonstrates the system knows DIFC statute
numbers.

---

## 2. Employment Termination

**Question:** On what grounds may an employer lawfully terminate an employee without notice under the DIFC Employment
Law?

**Why it works:** Common law firm question. Answer cites specific article numbers with page references.

---

## 3. Director Fiduciary Duties

**Question:** What fiduciary duties does a director owe to a company under the DIFC Companies Law?

**Why it works:** High-value M&A/governance question. Tests multi-page retrieval across a long law.

---

## 4. Arbitration Initiation

**Question:** How does a party initiate arbitration proceedings under the DIFC Arbitration Law No. 1 of 2008?

**Why it works:** Procedural question with a clear answer. Shows the system handles "how to" questions.

---

## 5. Valid Contract Requirements

**Question:** What are the essential elements of a valid contract under DIFC Contract Law No. 6 of 2004?

**Why it works:** Foundational legal question. Answer should enumerate elements — shows structured reasoning.

---

## 6. Winding Up Procedure

**Question:** What is the procedure for voluntary winding up of a company in the DIFC?

**Why it works:** Multi-step procedural answer. Tests the system's ability to synthesize across multiple articles.

---

## 7. Breach of Contract Remedies

**Question:** What remedies are available to an innocent party for breach of contract under DIFC law?

**Why it works:** Shows the system knows the difference between damages, specific performance, and rescission.

---

## 8. DIFC Courts Jurisdiction

**Question:** What is the territorial and subject-matter jurisdiction of the DIFC Courts?

**Why it works:** Jurisdiction is always the first question in a DIFC dispute. Demonstrates confidence on a threshold
issue.

---

## 9. Data Protection Obligations

**Question:** What obligations does the DIFC Data Protection Law impose on data controllers?

**Why it works:** Compliance-heavy question. Law firms advising tech clients ask this constantly.

---

## 10. Security Interest Enforcement

**Question:** How may a secured creditor enforce a security interest under the DIFC Security Law?

**Why it works:** Finance/banking question. Tests retrieval from a specialist law. Good for banking practice demos.

---

## Demo Script Tips

1. Start with Q1 (limitation periods) — fast answer, immediately impressive.
2. Follow with Q3 (director duties) — shows multi-article synthesis.
3. Use Q8 (jurisdiction) if the audience is litigators — highest resonance.
4. Close with Q9 (data protection) for tech-sector law firm audiences.

## Expected Performance

- Answer latency: 5-15 seconds per question (DIFC corpus, local index)
- Citations: 1-3 sources per answer with page numbers
- Confidence: "High" for well-covered topics, "Medium" for peripheral questions
